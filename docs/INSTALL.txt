WheelFlip — Start here / 安装说明
macOS 14+ · Apple silicon + Intel

English

1. Drag WheelFlip.app onto Applications. If you cannot install there, use your
   personal ~/Applications folder. Quit an older copy before replacing it.
2. Open the INSTALLED copy, then eject this disk. Look for WheelFlip in the menu
   bar, not the Dock.
3. This release is ad-hoc signed and is NOT Apple-notarized. If macOS cannot
   verify it, and you trust the release, follow Apple's app-specific Open Anyway
   flow in System Settings > Privacy & Security after trying to open it.
   https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
4. In WheelFlip's permission guide, click Open System Settings to open
   Accessibility, then Show This App in Finder to locate the correct app.
5. Click + in Accessibility, select that installed copy, and turn its switch on.
   Complete any password or Touch ID confirmation in the macOS dialog.
6. Click Reopen and Check in WheelFlip. The panel should say Enabled. Try your
   mouse wheel and trackpad; keep Natural scrolling on for the intended setup.

Pause keeps your rules and login item. Quit stops the service and removes its
login item. Updating may require adding the new copy to Accessibility again.
The source, checksums, and an agent installation prompt are available at:
https://github.com/WoWJony/wheelflip

简体中文

1. 把 WheelFlip.app 拖到 Applications（应用程序）。没有权限时可放到自己的
   ~/Applications 文件夹。升级前先退出旧副本。
2. 打开安装好的副本，然后推出这个磁盘。应用图标在菜单栏，不在 Dock。
3. 当前版本为临时签名，尚未经过 Apple 公证。首次打开若提示无法验证，确认
   信任后，在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」点击此应用对应的「仍要打开」。
   Apple 操作说明：https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
4. 在 WheelFlip 授权页面点「打开系统设置…」直达辅助功能，再点
   「在 Finder 中显示此应用」定位正确副本。
5. 在辅助功能中点「+」，选中刚安装的 WheelFlip.app 并开启开关。
   如需密码或 Touch ID，直接在 macOS 弹窗中完成。
6. 回到 WheelFlip 点「重新打开并检查」。面板应显示「已启用」。试试鼠标滚轮
   和触控板；若希望触控板自然滚动、鼠标传统方向，请保持系统自然滚动开启。

「暂停」保留规则和登录自启；「退出」停止服务并注销登录自启。
升级后可能需要重新添加辅助功能授权。仓库还提供可复制给 Agent 的安装向导：
https://github.com/WoWJony/wheelflip/blob/main/README.zh-CN.md
